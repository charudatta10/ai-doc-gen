Here are five traditional haiku poems about the moon, capturing moments of dream and reflection:

Silver crescent glows
Night's dark veil upon my soul
Peaceful, gentle light

Moonbeams dance on sea
Gentle waves caress the shore
Moon's soft, silvery kiss

Luna's soft delight
Morning dew upon her face
Summer's subtle charm

Starry night above
Twinkling diamonds in the sky
Dreams unfold like petals

Silent moon ascends
Shadows dance upon the wall
Midnight's quiet hush
